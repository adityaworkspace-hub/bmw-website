Rolling Canvas Heritage

The Legendary BMW Art Cars

Since 1975, BMW has united world-renowned visionary artists with high-speed endurance motorsport to transform racing legends into rolling works of modern art.

🎨 1979
Andy Warhol

BMW M1 Group 4 (1979)

Painted by hand in under 28 minutes. Warhol applied 6 kilos of vivid paint: "I attempted to show speed as a visual; when a car is going fast, all lines and colors become a blur." Placed 6th overall at 24 Hours of Le Mans.

⚡ 2010
Jeff Koons

BMW M3 GT2 (2010)

Exploding with cosmic contrast and vivid light trails, Koons drew inspiration from supersonic motion, debris bursts, and raw horsepower to manifest pure kinetic energy on the circuit.

🏎️ 1975
Alexander Calder

BMW 3.0 CSL (1975)

The spark that ignited the entire Art Car phenomenon. American sculptor Calder infused the muscular "Batmobile" CSL with primary red, blue, and yellow geometric blocks that energized Le Mans spectators.
